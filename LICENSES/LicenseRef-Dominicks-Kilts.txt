Terms of the Dominick's Finer Foods data
James M. Kilts Center for Marketing, University of Chicago Booth School of Business
Source: https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/kilts/research-data/dominicks (October 2026)

These terms apply to causaldemand/dominicks_brands/brands_cer.csv and causaldemand/dominicks_brands/brands_sna.csv,
which list product codes (UPCs) and brand names from the Dominick's data, and to the datasets that causaldemand builds
from the Dominick's data. They are not covered by the Apache License 2.0.
